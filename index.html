Carson

A private assistant for one family.

Carson is a personal, non-commercial application used by a single household. It helps the parents keep track of their calendar, their shared task list and the small day-to-day decisions of family life, through ordinary conversation.

Carson runs on a computer in the family's home. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and collects nothing from visitors to this page.

With the family's explicit permission, Carson reads their own calendar and task list so it can answer questions about their week. It has read-only access and cannot create, change or delete anything in those accounts.

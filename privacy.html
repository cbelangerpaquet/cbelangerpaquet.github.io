Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Carson is a private, non-commercial application used by a single family. It has no public users and no sign-up. This notice describes what Carson does with the data of the family members who choose to connect their own accounts to it.

What Carson accesses

Only with the account holder's explicit consent, Carson reads:

  • calendar events, in order to answer questions about the family's week;
  • task lists, so existing tasks do not have to be retyped.

Access is read-only. Carson requests no permission to create, modify or delete anything, so it cannot do so.

Where the data goes

Carson runs on a computer in the family's home. Calendar and task information is stored only on that computer, in the household's own files. Sign-in credentials are stored on that computer alone and are deliberately excluded from the household's backups.

Data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train any model. It is not published or transmitted anywhere outside the household, other than to the service the account belongs to when reading it.

Retention and deletion

The local copy is refreshed regularly and is replaced by newer data. An account holder can revoke Carson's access at any time from their own account's security settings, after which Carson stops reading it. Deleting the application's local files removes the stored copy entirely.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to the address listed as the developer contact on the application's consent screen.
